I've been using a launcher app of some sort since the [Quicksilver](https://qsapp.com) days. I used [Alfred](https://www.alfredapp.com) for a while, but Raycast [caught my attention](https://www.platformer.news/ai-productivity-apps-capacities-raycast-readwise/) about two years ago. It's truly a fantastic app, the logical next step in my app switcher journey. 

I use Raycast probably dozens if not hundreds of times a day, for everything from app swithcing and quick web searches, to simple snippets, color picking, calulating, generating Lorem Ipsum text and more. You can even run shell commands right in the Raycast window.

I am slightly concerned about how AI-forward the app has become over the last year. It's easy enough to turn off, but it very clearly is a direction the company is prioritizing.
