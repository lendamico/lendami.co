Sindre Sorhus is the kind of developer we don't see too often these days: he makes tiny apps that do one thing incredibly well, and offers them for a fair one-time price (or free).

Here are a few of my favorites:
- [Gifski](https://sindresorhus.com/gifski): Convert videos to high-quality GIFs
- [Lungo](https://sindresorhus.com/lungo): Prevent your Mac from going to sleep
- [Pasteboard Viewer](https://sindresorhus.com/pasteboard-viewer): Inspect the system pasteboards
- [Pure Paste](https://sindresorhus.com/pure-paste): Paste as plain text by default
- [Shareful](https://sindresorhus.com/shareful): Give the macOS share menu superpowers
- [Velja](https://sindresorhus.com/velja): Powerful browser picker
